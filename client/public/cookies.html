Cookie Policy

A short, honest list of the cookies and storage entries used on this site, what they do, and how to switch them off.

Last updated: 2026-05-05

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files a website asks your browser to keep. localStorage is a similar idea — small bits of text saved per-site, not sent to the server. Both are used to remember settings (like your nickname) and, in some cases, for advertising. We treat both the same in this policy, even though only "cookies" are technically named.

Categories we use

  • Strictly necessary — required for the site to function (your seat in a room, the consent decision itself). We don't ask consent for these because the site cannot work without them.
  • Advertising / personalization — set by Google AdSense and the Google Funding Choices consent platform. Loaded only after you accept in the consent dialog.

We do not currently use analytics or A/B-testing cookies.
